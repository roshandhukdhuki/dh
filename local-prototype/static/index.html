What this score means

Up to 50 for brevity, 20 for a vowel ratio of 20–60%, 10 for no repeated letters, minus 10 for four consecutive consonants. Maximum 80. This is a sorting aid, not an appraisal.

Truthful availabilityAll imported and generated names start unknown. CSV availability claims are ignored. A registrar response, timestamp and price check are needed before deciding to buy. No provider is connected.
Language dataNepali and Sanskrit original text is retained separately from the ASCII domain spelling. Historical meanings are not independently verified. Live Datamuse suggestions and Nepali Wiktionary titles are recorded with provenance. Mechanical Nepali romanization needs human review; meanings are not invented.
Runtime & provider limitsThe optional source worker makes at most one Datamuse and one Nepali Wiktionary request per 10-minute cycle, plus 40 local brandables. Registrar API calls remain disabled. It stops if this computer or process stops. No 24/7 uptime guarantee.
